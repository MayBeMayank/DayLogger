# Future scopes

Ideas for top-level features, not yet committed to.

## Priority

1. **Charts & analysis**: spend by category, subcategory, tag and person over a date range. The data model already supports it.
2. **Budgets**: monthly limit per category, with a warning near the limit.
3. **Recurring entries**: auto-add rent, subscriptions and EMIs.

## Other ideas

- **Search & filter**: search all entries by title, tag, note, amount or person.
- **Journal/notes**: a daily mood and note log.
- **Habit stats**: streak history, best streak, weekly/monthly completion.
- **Home widgets**: Android widget to tick a streak or add an entry.
- **CSV/PDF export**: for spreadsheets and tax records.
- **App lock**: PIN or biometric.
- **Cloud backup**: optional auto-backup to Drive/OneDrive.
- **Multi-currency**: currency per entry or category.
- **Year in review**: a shareable summary card, extending `ShareCardRenderer`.
